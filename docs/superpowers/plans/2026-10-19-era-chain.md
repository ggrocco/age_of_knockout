# Era Chain (Medieval → Modern) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add Medieval Age, Age of Sail, Industrial Age and Modern Age between Iron and AI Future. Each era gets its land
units, the Castle and the Factory, per-era building looks, bot support, and a Death Match that starts in Iron.

**Architecture:** Eras are data (`src/data/ages.js`, `units.js`, `buildings.js`). The sim already gates on that data
through `ageUpNeed`/`ageUpProblem`. The bot gains one generic "build what the gate needs" rule and longer preference
lists. Rendering reads a new pure-data `src/render/styles.js` for building looks and adds unit builders to `models.js`.
Balance is measured headlessly (Task 6) before any 3D art (Tasks 7–8).

**Tech Stack:** Plain ES modules, Node test runner (`node --test`), three.js procedural models. No build step, no new
dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-era-chain-design.md` (§ numbers below refer to it).

## Global Constraints

- `src/sim` and `src/ai` stay DOM-free and deterministic: seeded RNG only, no `Math.random`, no DOM.
- No new dependencies and no downloaded assets. All art is procedural, and nothing is derived from Age of Empires art.
- Era sentence names (`AGE_TITLE`), verbatim:
  - stone `Stone Age`, tool `Tool Age`, bronze `Bronze Age`, iron `Iron Age`;
  - medieval `Medieval Age`, sail `Age of Sail`, industrial `Industrial Age`, modern `Modern Age`;
  - future `AI Future Age`.
- Short names (`AGE_NAME`) gain medieval `Medieval`, sail `Age of Sail`, industrial `Industrial`, modern `Modern`.
- No UI text may contain "Sail Age", "Age of Sail Age" or a bare "Sail".
- Unit, building and age-up numbers come verbatim from spec §1–§2. Only Task 6 may change age-up costs and times and the
  Death Match wave timing, and every change is recorded in `progress.md`.
- Match the surrounding style: one-line data entries, existing helper names (`c()`, `B`, `cached`, `pivot`, `mesh`),
  and the same sparse comments.
- `DESC` text is 20–200 characters and claims only what the sim does.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Work on branch `claude/era-chain`, and never
  push without asking the user.
- Every task ends with `npm test` fully green.
